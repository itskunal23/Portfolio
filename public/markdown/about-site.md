# About This Site

This portfolio is a macOS-style desktop. Open apps from the dock:

- **Bear**: my profile and full project case studies (you're reading it now).
- **Terminal**: browse my background as a tiny filesystem (`ls`, `cd about`, `cat
  bio.txt`).
- **Safari**: contact and links.
- **Launchpad**: jump straight to a case study or my résumé.

It's built with [React](https://react.dev/), [Zustand](https://zustand-demo.pmnd.rs/),
[UnoCSS](https://unocss.dev/), [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/), and
[Vite](https://vitejs.dev/).

The macOS desktop interface is adapted from the open-source
[playground-macos](https://github.com/Renovamen/playground-macos) template by Renovamen
(MIT licensed). All content, project case studies, and architecture diagrams here are my
own work.

## A note on the project case studies

The projects describe real engineering I've done in professional roles. To protect
proprietary information, the case studies use **generalized architecture** and contain no
internal source code, confidential data, restricted screenshots, internal URLs, or
credentials. Architecture diagrams are **conceptual explanations** of the systems, not
reproductions of internal designs.
