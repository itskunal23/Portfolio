## Graduate study

I'm in the **Georgia Tech OMSCS** program, specializing in **Artificial Intelligence**.

Current course: **Knowledge-Based AI (KBAI)** with David Joyner and Ashok Goel. KBAI covers
how to design agents that reason with structured knowledge: semantic networks, frames,
means-ends analysis, case-based reasoning, planning, and production systems. It maps
directly to the work I do, where deterministic reasoning and explicit structure matter as
much as the model.

## How I build

- The model is one component. Retrieval, orchestration, validation, and observability
  decide whether a system is reliable.
- In enterprise RAG, retrieval quality drives answer quality. Hybrid search, reranking,
  and grounded citations do more than a larger model.
- Agentic systems need determinism where it counts: explicit execution paths, confidence
  thresholds, and defined handoff to a human.
- Tracing, metrics, and LLM-as-a-judge evaluation make a system measurable and defensible.
